About SkillNetz

Where creativity meets opportunity

We're building the friendliest freelancing marketplace in India — a place where talented people find meaningful work and businesses find the partners they need to grow.

Our Story

Born from a simple idea

SkillNetz started with a question: why is finding good work — or good talent — so hard? Traditional job boards feel cold, and big marketplaces feel impersonal. We wanted to build something warmer.

Today, SkillNetz is a dynamic space that connects talented freelancers with clients seeking top-notch services. We believe in empowering freelancers to showcase their skills and enabling businesses to find the right talent for their projects — with a community feel that keeps people coming back.
